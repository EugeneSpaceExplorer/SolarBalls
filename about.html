About This Project

🚀 Your Universe HQ

A personal, SolarBalls-inspired astronomy website designed around exploration, stories and interactive learning.

🌌 Design goals

Dark cosmic visuals, big readable cards, simple navigation and a clear path from an interactive object to its own information page.

🧠 Learning goals

The site connects fictional character-style storytelling with real astronomy concepts without presenting fan material as official.

🎮 Game

The Universe Quiz uses a 60-question bank and selects 10 questions for each round. Scores are stored locally in the browser.

Made as a personal SolarBalls-inspired astronomy project. This is an unofficial fan site and is not affiliated with the creators of SolarBalls.
