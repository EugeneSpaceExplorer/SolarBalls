🌌 A Quick Solar System Tour

Our Solar System is a tiny neighborhood inside a galaxy containing hundreds of billions of stars.

☀️
Sun

Our star, containing almost all the Solar System's mass.

🌍
Inner planets

Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars are rocky worlds.

🪐
Outer planets

Jupiter and Saturn are gas giants; Uranus and Neptune are ice giants.

🌑
Moons

Many planets have moons, including some fascinating worlds.

☄️
Small bodies

Asteroids, comets, dwarf planets, and smaller objects fill the system.

🛰️
Beyond Neptune

The Solar System continues into distant regions such as the Kuiper Belt.

🌍 Keep looking up. The universe is waiting. 🚀
