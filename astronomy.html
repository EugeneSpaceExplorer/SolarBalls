Learn the Cosmos

🔭 Astronomy

A friendly starting point for understanding the science behind the SolarBalls-style stories.

🌟 Stars

Stars are enormous balls of hot plasma powered mainly by nuclear fusion. Their mass affects their temperature, lifetime and final fate.

🌌 Galaxies

Galaxies are huge gravitational systems containing stars, gas, dust and dark matter. Our Solar System lives in the Milky Way.

🕳️ Black holes

Black holes are regions where gravity is so strong that, past the event horizon, even light cannot escape.

💫 Cosmology

Cosmology studies the universe as a whole, including its expansion, history and large-scale structure.

🚀 Space travel

Orbits, gravity assists, rockets and light-speed limits all shape how we explore space.

🪐 Exoplanets

Exoplanets are worlds orbiting stars beyond our Sun. Astronomers can detect many using methods such as transits and radial velocity.
