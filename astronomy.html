🔭 The Science Behind the Fun

The real universe is already spectacular.

☀️ Light travel: Sunlight takes about 8 minutes 20 seconds to reach Earth.
🌌 Space is huge: The distances between planets are enormous, and outer-system spacecraft can take years to travel there.
🛰️ Gravity: Gravity keeps planets orbiting stars and moons orbiting planets.

🌑 Moons aren't boring

Moons can have ice, oceans, atmospheres, volcanoes, and complicated histories. Europa and Titan are two famous examples.

✨ The big idea

Astronomy is more than memorizing names. It is asking why things move, how worlds form, and what might exist beyond what we can observe.
